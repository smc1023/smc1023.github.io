Privacy Policy — Physical AI Uploader

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Physical AI Uploader (the "App"), a private tool operated by the owner of the Physical AI & Robotics News channel (the "Owner"), handles information.

1. Summary

The App is used only by the Owner to upload the Owner's own videos to the Owner's own TikTok and YouTube accounts as drafts for review. It does not collect, sell or share personal data about anyone else.

2. Information the App accesses

When the Owner connects an account, the platform provides the App with:

  • Access tokens that let the App upload videos to the Owner's account (for TikTok, the video.upload permission; for YouTube, upload and channel-management permissions).
  • Basic account information (such as the account's display name and ID), used only to confirm the correct account is connected.

The App does not access followers, messages, contacts, viewing history, payment details or any other users' data.

3. How the information is used

Access tokens and basic account information are used solely to upload the Owner's videos as drafts or private videos, and to confirm which account is connected. They are not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

4. Storage and security

Tokens are stored locally on the Owner's personal computer, in private files that are not shared or uploaded anywhere. No database or server stores this information. Tokens can be deleted at any time by deleting those files or revoking access (see section 6).

5. Sharing

The App does not sell, rent or share any information with third parties. Data is exchanged only with TikTok and YouTube (Google), through their official APIs, as needed to upload videos.

6. Your choices

Access can be revoked at any time:

  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services → remove the App.
  • YouTube / Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions → remove the App.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children and is not used by anyone other than the Owner.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
